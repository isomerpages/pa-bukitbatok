<h4>Bukit Batok Citizens’ Consultative Committees (CCC)</h4>
<p></p>
<p>The Bukit Batok Citizens’ Consultative Committees (CCC) serves as a crucial
link between the community and the Government. The CCC is responsible for
organising and spearheading various significant grassroots initiatives
within the constituency, supervising local aid programs, and coordinating
large-scale fundraising efforts and national campaigns.</p>
<p></p>
<h4>Programmes</h4>
<p>The CCC organises various programmes to support <a href="https://www.pa.gov.sg/about-us/about-pa" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" target="_blank"><u>PA’s mission</u></a>
</p>
<h4>Outreach Programmes</h4>
<p>These programmes are designed to:</p>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>Build strong community ties with events such as festival celebrations;</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Encourage residents to get to know and understand one another; and</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Support the Government and various agencies in nationwide campaigns such
as dengue prevention, Clean &amp; Green Week, Racial Harmony Month and
Good Neighbour Day.</p>
</li>
</ul>
<h4>Assistance Programmes</h4>
<p>CCCs help implement/ administer assistance schemes for the needy and economically
underprivileged in our community. These schemes include:</p>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>CCCs ComCare Fund for urgent, temporary relief in hardship cases;</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Edusave Awards;</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Pre-School Outreach programmes; and</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Distribution of adhoc meal/ transport vouchers.</p>
</li>
</ul>
<h4>Bridging Programmes</h4>
<p>CCCs facilitate interaction between the people and the Government by explaining
government policies and gathering feedback from residents. These bridging
programmes include:</p>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>Community forums involving residents; and</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Dialogue sessions with representatives from the Government agencies.</p>
</li>
</ul>
