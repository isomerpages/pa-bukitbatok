<h1><strong>2023 – Present</strong></h1>

<p>West Winds celebrates 30 years of music making since its humble beginnings
in 1993. What started as a place for people from all walks of life to come
together and share their common passion for band music, has grown and developed
into a community with a strong local presence. This concert will be a special
one for the audience and our members alike as we look back on the various
concert themes explored by the band throughout the years. Our repertoire
will feature pieces like Music for a Festival, Traveler, East Coast Pictures,
Magellan’s Voyage to Unknown Continent and Kungfu Panda. Join us as we
embark on an Epic Journey through West Wind’s history!</p>

<p><strong>West Winds in Concert 2023 II</strong>
<br>Spartacus
<br>Guest Conductor – Jan van der Roost
<br>European wind music today is characterised by a generation of composers
who, in the last decades, have enriched this repertoire with interesting
original compositions. One of the most important of these composers is
undoubtedly Jan Van der Roost.</p>
<p>
<br>Catch renowned Belgian composer, Jan Van der Roost in action as he conducts
West Winds for the first time in</p>
<p>
<br>Singapore! He is the composer behind compositions such as the Roman epic,&nbsp;<em>Spartacus</em>,
and the well-
<br>loved&nbsp;<em>Singapura Suite</em>. His musical creations have touched
scores of musicians locally and worldwide.
<br>Other repertoire will feature pieces like <em>Resplendent Glory</em> by
Rossano Galante, <em>Hymn to the Sun – with the beat of Mother Earth</em> by
Satoshi Yagisawa conducted by ME6 Philip Tng.</p>
<p><strong>Programme</strong>
<br>Resplendent Glory – Rossano Galante

<br>Flying Jewels – James David
<br>Takayama Impressions – Jan van der Roost
<br>Kebek – Jan van der Roost
<br>Adagio for Winds – Jan van der Roost

<p>Embark on an adventurous tale with the West Winds as they take you on
a musical storytelling like no other, featuring guest conductor Daisuke
Shimizu from Japan! Shimizu is a renowned conductor and composer, having
composed music for prestigious ensembles such as the Japan Central Air
Defense Force Band and the Osaka Shion Wind Orchestra. Highlights include
his Sea of Wisdom and Adventure Tale of Professor Alex, as well as The
Skyscrapers!&nbsp;</p>
<p>The concert will also feature other exciting repertoire conducted by ME6
Philip Tng, including the epic suite from Williams’ Star Wars – The Force
Awakens and Smith’s Songs of Sailor and Sea. Don’t miss out on this incredible
opportunity to experience the magic of West Winds and Daisuke Shimizu with
an evening of enjoyable Band music!&nbsp;</p>
<p>With such a diverse range of music on offer, there is sure to be something
for everyone at this concert. So come and join us for an evening of enjoyable
Band music and let Daisuke Shimizu and West Winds and transport you to
a world of wonder and enchantment.&nbsp;&nbsp;</p>
